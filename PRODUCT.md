# FieldTrack

FieldTrack is an internal workplace operations tool for office staff who record and report satellite and internet installations completed by contractors across the Philippines.

The product should feel dependable, compact, and calm. Staff may use it for repetitive encoding during a normal office day, so the interface favors clear labels, familiar controls, readable tables, and fast filtering over decoration. Admins need enough traceability to answer operational questions without returning to scattered spreadsheets.

The first release supports admin and staff roles, installation lifecycle tracking, Philippine location fields, detailed equipment components, Excel import, CSV export, optional photo evidence, dashboard summaries, and basic audit metadata.
